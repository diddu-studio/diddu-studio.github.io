Information Collection

To provide a better user experience while using our applications, we may need to request access to certain device permissions.

We only request permissions that are strictly necessary for the application's functionality — nothing more.

Permissions that our applications may request include:

  • Internet Access: Used to load advertisements and ensure up-to-date content is available.
  • Internal Storage: Some applications require this permission to store local data for better performance.
  • Record Audio: Only for applications that feature audio recording functionality. Other apps do not require this permission.

Please note that not all apps require all of the above permissions. Each application only requests permissions relevant to its specific features and functionality.

🔒

Information Security

User data security is our top priority. We follow strict principles in handling all data:

  • Internet connection is only used to load advertisements — no user data is transmitted over the network.
  • Data stored on the user's device is used solely for the functional needs of the application.
  • We never send user data to any third parties.
  • We guarantee that no data is written to the device that is unrelated to the application's purpose.
Your privacy — and your child's privacy — is safe with us. We do not sell, rent, or share any personal data with anyone.
📢

Advertisement

All our products are free to download and install. To sustain this free service, our applications display advertisements.

We deeply care about the safety of content displayed to children. Therefore, we apply a strict advertisement content filter:

  • All ads displayed go through a filtering process to ensure they are appropriate for all ages of children.
  • Ads containing adult content, violence, or anything unsuitable for children are automatically blocked.
  • We partner with advertising networks that maintain high standards of child protection.
By downloading and using our apps, users consent to the display of filtered, child-appropriate advertisements.
